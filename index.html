About Me

Hi! I am Basil K Aji, an AI Engineer based in Kerala, India. I completed my Masters in Artificial Intelligence from Amrita University and hold a Bachelor's degree from Mahatma Gandhi University (MG University).


I have a huge interest in Amodal Research — the fascinating challenge of enabling machines to perceive and reason about parts of objects that are hidden or occluded from view. I love diving deep into how vision systems can go beyond what is directly visible to build complete, holistic scene understanding, and I am actively working on projects at the intersection of amodal perception and deep learning.


Are you also passionate about Computer Vision? Working on CV research, building CV systems, or just curious about the field? I would love to connect!
